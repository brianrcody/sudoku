What to look at

Q1. Switch Option A / B and compare the right column with and without the panel, on wide and narrow frames. In B, open the menu and choose Statistics….

Interactions

  • Menu: click, or focus the button and press Enter, Space, ↓ or ↑. Then ↑/↓, Home/End, letter keys, Enter/Space. Esc or Tab closes it.
  • Theme and Left-handed apply at once and leave the menu open. Statistics, Share and Reset close it.
  • Reset… opens the existing confirmation. Cancel or confirm returns focus to the menu button.
  • Share: tick "No puzzle" to see it disabled. It stays focusable and reads as dimmed.
  • New Puzzle confirms because the board is in progress. "Show win" puts focus on it (R3).
  • Narrow frame: Left-handed leaves the menu (R6). The menu stays a dropdown with 44px rows, and the button stays at the right because handedness has no effect when stacked.
