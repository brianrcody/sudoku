If the search budget is exhausted, the player is told the truth and chooses. "Play" loads the found puzzle labeled with its true tier; Cancel restores the prior state. Uses the existing dialog component.

4 · Coach — Finned X-Wing (Expert, full coaching)

Existing treatments throughout, plus the one new cell role: the fin (mid-purple fill + dotted ring + "fin" tag — distinguishable without color). The X-Wing rectangle uses the existing connector outline; dashed pointers mark eliminations.

Finned X-Wing

4 almost forms an X-Wing — except for the fin. Either the X-Wing holds, or the fin is 4. Both ways, 4 can't appear in cells covered by both.

5 · Coach — Unique Rectangle Type 1 (Diabolical, full coaching)

The four rectangle cells get the cause treatment joined by a closed connector outline; the roof corner is also an elimination target (dashed ring). Fully walkable — this is the flagship coaching experience of the new Diabolical tier.

Unique Rectangle

If these four cells held only 3 and 7, the puzzle would have two solutions — and every puzzle here has exactly one. The corner with extra candidates can't be just 3/7: remove 3 and 7 from it.

6 · Coach — ALS-XZ (Nightmare, limited coaching)

Limited fidelity: the two almost-locked sets reuse the coloring group treatments (Group A solid, Group B dashed), eliminations are marked, the rule is stated, and the note says the rest is yours. No arrows, no walk-through — by design.

ALS-XZ

These two groups are each one digit short of locked. They share 2 restrictively — so one group must contain 9. Cells seeing every 9 in both groups can't contain 9.

Tracing which digits lock each group is an advanced exercise — the highlights show the two groups and the result.
